About Leaderboards

A community-driven ranking platform where anyone can create a leaderboard on anything — and let the crowd decide what rises to the top.

The Idea

Born from a simple question: "What if voting felt as addictive as a leaderboard?" We combined competitive ranking mechanics with open community curation to create a space where opinions become data.

Creator

J
Jared
Founder & Developer

Philosophy

Every opinion deserves a rank. Every rank deserves a community. No ads, no pay-to-win — just votes.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

What We Collect

Discord username, avatar, and user ID on login. We store your votes, leaderboards, and reputation score. No passwords — auth is fully handled by Discord OAuth.

How We Use It

Solely to power your experience: profile, vote tracking, reputation. We never sell or share your data.

Cookies

One session cookie to keep you logged in. No tracking. No ads. Clear it anytime by logging out.

Your Rights

Request account deletion at any time. We process requests within 30 days.
